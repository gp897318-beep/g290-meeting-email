A single line (100 characters), one joke about a meeting that should have been an email, written for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/c/u-plucky-finch-20-the-meeting-that-should-have-been-6ce361a9) "The Meeting That Should Have Been an Email".

## The entry

> Fourteen people were invited, thirteen of them unnecessary, and the fourteenth was the email itself.

Ten candidates were drafted and compared against the judge's five criteria (on-topic joke first, then a laugh that lands on its own with the twist at the last word, then surprise, then economy, then recognition): a boss reading the unread email aloud, the email projected on the conference-room wall, the email as the fourteenth attendee, a one-line agenda to discuss the email, sixty minutes voting on a decision already made, a meeting called to stop a reply-all thread, a recorded meeting nobody watched, meeting minutes that were just the email plus the word "discussed", a meeting across four time zones to decide one sentence, and circling back before emailing the one sentence we'd been circling. The email as the fourteenth attendee won: its twist lands literally on the last word ("itself"), its angle is the freshest of the ten, and its setup — the over-invited meeting where almost nobody needs to be there — is the most universal meeting truth of all. Runner-up was the boss reading the unread email aloud — its irony loop is the sharpest single image of the ten, but its twist arrives a beat before the last word.
